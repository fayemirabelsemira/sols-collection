Privacy Policy

Last updated: [October 09, 2026]

Sol's Collection is operated by Faye Semira in the Philippines. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use this website, why, and what your rights are under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (the password is stored hashed by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
  • Reading data: your reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, votes, and the comments you post.
  • Purchase data: which chapters or books you bought, the amount, and the payment status. We do not see or store your card, e-wallet, or bank details. Those are handled by our payment processor.
  • Device data: settings such as your reading theme and font size are saved in your browser's local storage.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To unlock content you purchased and remember where you stopped reading.
  • To show vote and view counts and display your comments.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud, and respond to support requests.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the site: Supabase (database and authentication), PayMongo (payment processing), and Cloudflare (hosting). They process data only to provide their services. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, object to or withdraw consent for its processing, and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). To exercise any of these rights, email us at fayesemira21@gmail.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The site contains mature and dark romance themes and is intended for readers aged [16+]. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.
